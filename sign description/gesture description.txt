Here is the detailed mechanical breakdown for these 10 essential everyday ASL gestures, focusing on handshapes, starting positions, movements, and palm orientations.
------------------------------
## 1. HELLO

* Handshape: Flat hand (fingers pressed together, thumb tucked slightly).
* Starting Position: Bring your hand up to the side of your forehead, near your temple. Your palm should face forward or slightly downward.
* Movement: Make a small, crisp outward arc away from your temple, mimicking a formal salute. Keep it brief and intentional.
* Visual Anchor: A standard, respectful military-style salute but softened for conversation. [4, 5, 6] 

## 2. LOVE

* Handshape: Both hands form loose fists with palms facing inward toward your body.
* Starting Position: Cross both wrists directly over the center of your chest, forming an "X" shape.
* Movement: Press both arms firmly against your chest, hugging your torso tightly.
* Visual Anchor: The universal, instinctive motion of hugging someone close to your heart.

## 3. YES

* Handshape: Make an "S" fist (standard tight fist with your thumb folded flat across the front of your fingers).
* Starting Position: Hold your fist up at shoulder height, palm facing forward toward the listener.
* Movement: Bending strictly at the wrist, nod your fist up and down twice. Keep your forearm completely still.
* Visual Anchor: Your fist acts as a miniature head physically nodding "yes".

## 4. NO

* Handshape: Extend your index and middle fingers together, and extend your thumb. Curl your ring and pinky fingers flat into your palm.
* Starting Position: Hold your hand out in front of your shoulder, palm facing forward toward the listener.
* Movement: Snap your index and middle fingers down quickly to tap the pad of your thumb twice.
* Visual Anchor: The fingers look like a mouth rapidly biting down or snapping closed. [9] 

## 5. SORRY

* Handshape: Make an "A" fist (fingers tucked tightly, thumb pressed flat against the outside of your index finger index knuckle).
* Starting Position: Place the inner knuckle side of your fist directly against the center of your chest.
* Movement: Rub your fist in the exact same circular motion as "PLEASE" (clockwise circles over your chest) while expressing regret on your face.
* Visual Anchor: It is identical to "PLEASE," but using a heavy fist instead of an open hand to represent carrying a burden. [11] 

## 6. THANK YOU

* Handshape: Flat hand (fingers straight and pressed side-by-side).
* Starting Position: Touch the tips of your fingers flat against your lips or the front of your chin. Your palm faces inward toward your face.
* Movement: Move your hand forward and downward through the air toward the listener, ending with your palm facing upward.
* Visual Anchor: Physically blowing or tossing a token of appreciation from your lips directly to the other person. [12, 13, 14]
